A Cut Above: Mow & Grow - Mowing Property
Third-party art used in res://Assets/Foilage/ and res://Assets/Buildings/

KayKit Forest Nature Pack (1.0 FREE) - Kay Lousberg, www.kaylousberg.com - CC0 1.0
    Trees, bushes and rocks placed around every generated property, the shrubs
    planted in the lawn beds, and the shared forest palette texture they use.
    See KayKit_Forest_Nature_Pack_License.txt

Ultimate Textured Building Pack - @Quaternius, patreon.com/quaternius - CC0 1.0
    Eight house and institutional models, in res://Assets/Buildings/Low Poly/.
    They stand OUTSIDE the playable boundary on suburban and landscaped
    properties: the house at the end of the garden, the neighbours, and the
    building a set of grounds belongs to.

    These are the "_Mat" variants, which carry their own colours in an .mtl
    rather than sharing a palette texture. The copied .mtl files were converted
    from linear to sRGB, because Godot reads Kd straight into albedo_color and
    treats it as sRGB; no geometry was altered.

    The same pack is used by the Business Town. See
    Quaternius_Ultimate_Textured_Buildings_License.txt

Both packs are CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication); attribution is
appreciated but not required.

Everything else on a mowing property is generated by the game at runtime and
has no third-party source:

    the ground and the distant hills   a seeded height field
    the grass tufts                    built as meshes when the scene loads
    the distant tree stand-ins         built as meshes when the scene loads
    the grass, ground and water looks  shaders written for this project
